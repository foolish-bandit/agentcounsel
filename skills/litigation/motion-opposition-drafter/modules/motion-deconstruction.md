# Motion Deconstruction and Response Map

> Draft legal work product for attorney review. This module inherits every safety rule and gate in the parent skill.

Use this module in standard and deep-review modes.

## Deconstruct the movant's papers

Create one entry for each numbered heading or distinct proposition advanced by the movant. For every entry:

1. Restate the argument accurately in the movant's own terms and cite the exact page or paragraph in the supplied papers.
2. Record each cited case, statute, rule, regulation, or other authority exactly as presented.
3. Add `[VERIFY: confirm citation, confirm it says what the movant claims, confirm current treatment]` to every authority. Never infer that the authority exists, is correctly characterized, or remains current.
4. List each supporting factual assertion with a pinpoint to the movant's papers.
5. Flag ambiguity rather than resolving it into a weaker argument.

Where subsequent treatment may matter, identify the authority as a candidate for `skills/legal-research/negative-treatment-check/SKILL.md`.

## Build the point-by-point response map

For each movant argument, record:

- the client's counter-position;
- each supporting record item and pinpoint, or `[VERIFY: record cite needed]`;
- the type of response authority required, followed by `[citation needed]`;
- whether the gap is directed authority still to be supplied or a genuine research referral to `skills/legal-research/legal-research-memo/SKILL.md`;
- any threshold, procedural, standard-of-review, or timeliness point, without asserting an unprovided rule and without calculating a deadline;
- the most likely rejoinder and any record or authority needed to answer it.

## Confirm posture and presentation constraints

Record the motion type, requested relief, supplied standard of review, supplied burden, hearing or response date, and forum requirements. Mark anything not directly supplied `[CONFIRM: attorney to verify]`; mark every timing item `[deadline verification required]`.

## Surface weakness before prose drafting

For each weak, unsupported, internally inconsistent, or strategically risky response point:

- explain the weakness in the Drafting Notes;
- identify the missing evidence or authority;
- give at least two attorney options, ordinarily pursue with an explicit caveat, reframe, omit, or research;
- never dress a weak response up as settled or well supported.

## Drafting Notes structure

Populate Sections 1 through 4 of `templates/opposition-response-outline.md` with:

- motion summary and procedural posture;
- argument deconstruction;
- movant-authority verification inventory;
- point-by-point response map;
- record gaps;
- response-authority gaps and research referrals;
- standard-of-review and forum-rule confirmations;
- weak points, attorney options, and open questions.
