# Opposition Drafting

> Draft legal work product for attorney review. This module inherits every safety rule and gate in the parent skill.

Use this module after `motion-deconstruction.md` in standard and deep-review modes.

## Drafting disciplines

Draft the opposition from the approved response map while applying all four disciplines at the same time:

1. **Accurate characterization.** Fairly restate the movant's argument before answering it. Preserve material qualifications and the actual requested relief.
2. **Record fidelity.** Cite every factual statement to a supplied record item with the available page, line, paragraph, exhibit, or Bates identifier. Use `[VERIFY: record cite needed]` where support is absent. Quote exactly; flag any paraphrase.
3. **Citation discipline.** Add no new authority. Mark every response-side proposition `[citation needed]` or `[pin cite needed]`. Keep the complete verification label on every movant authority discussed.
4. **Candor about weakness.** Flag an unsupported or risky response point inline with `[NOTE TO ATTORNEY: see Drafting Notes item ___]`.

## Opposition draft structure

Begin with:

> **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW - DO NOT FILE UNREVIEWED**
> Every response citation, movant authority, quotation, paraphrase, factual statement, record pinpoint, procedural rule, and date requires attorney verification before filing or transmission.

Then produce:

- a preliminary statement accurately summarizing the client's opposition position;
- the standard of review as supplied by counsel or `[standard of review: verify jurisdiction]`;
- response headings that track the movant's sequence unless the attorney has approved another organization;
- a fair statement of each movant point followed by the corresponding response;
- supplied record citations or visible record gaps;
- directed authority or visible citation and research gaps;
- inline weak-point notes;
- `[deadline verification required]` on the response deadline and every other timing item;
- `[CONFIRM: page/word limit not verified]` and equivalent placeholders for unsupplied forum requirements.

Do not convert the draft into a file-ready pleading. Signature blocks, final caption details, service language, filing metadata, and jurisdiction-specific certification language remain attorney-controlled unless supplied and confirmed.
