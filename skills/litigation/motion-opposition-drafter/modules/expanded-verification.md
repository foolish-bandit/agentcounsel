# Expanded Filing Verification

> Draft legal work product for attorney review. This module inherits every safety rule and gate in the parent skill.

Use this module only in deep-review mode. Add these checks to the parent checklist and pre-populate every unresolved item.

- [ ] Each supporting declaration, affidavit, exhibit, transcript, pleading, and cited docket item was reviewed in full.
- [ ] Every argument in the motion deconstruction matches the actual movant papers, including qualifications, exceptions, and requested relief.
- [ ] Every movant authority was retrieved and checked for citation accuracy, quoted language, holding, context, and current treatment, directly or through `negative-treatment-check`.
- [ ] Every response-side legal proposition is supported by verified primary authority selected by the attorney.
- [ ] Every pinpoint citation resolves to the asserted proposition or record fact.
- [ ] Every quotation was checked character by character against the supplied source.
- [ ] Every paraphrase was checked against the source and revised if it changes meaning.
- [ ] Every factual assertion is tied to provided record material; unsupported assertions were removed or expressly identified.
- [ ] Any evidentiary foundation, authentication, admissibility, objection, or judicial-notice issue implicated by the response has been assessed by counsel.
- [ ] Standard of review, burdens, threshold requirements, procedural posture, and available relief are correct for the forum and motion type. `[verify jurisdiction]`
- [ ] Local rules, standing orders, judge-specific procedures, citation form, filing format, page or word limits, and service requirements have been independently confirmed.
- [ ] The response deadline, hearing date, and every related timing item were independently verified from authoritative sources; no date was calculated by this workflow. `[deadline verification required]`
- [ ] All genuine research referrals were completed outside this drafting workflow and incorporated by the attorney.
- [ ] Adverse authority and the strongest version of the movant's position were considered rather than omitted or weakened.
- [ ] Every weak response point was affirmatively accepted, reframed, researched, or removed by the responsible attorney.
- [ ] Duty of candor, factual contentions, legal-contention support, sanctions exposure, and any certification obligations were assessed by counsel.
- [ ] Confidential, privileged, sealed, redacted, and protective-order material is handled consistently with governing restrictions.
- [ ] The final draft is internally consistent with the case theory, record, requested relief, and other filed positions.
- [ ] All open questions and placeholders are resolved before filing.
- [ ] The responsible attorney has performed the final substantive and filing review and accepts professional responsibility for the opposition.
